01 — The problem

A shop floor runs on memory. Memory forgets the back rack.

Nobody in the shop is doing anything wrong. There has simply been no way to match the right piece to the right person at the moment they walk in.

  1. i

    The shopper waits, tries, tires.

    Pieces come out one at a time, chosen by guesswork. Some suit, most do not, and the decision is often made on patience rather than on what looked best.

  2. ii

    The salesperson brings what they remember.

    That means the newest arrivals and the nearest racks. A piece that would suit this shopper perfectly stays folded at the back because no one thought of it in time.

  3. iii

    The owner watches stock age quietly.

    Discounting is the only lever, and it cuts margin on everything. After a sale, there is no way to tell what the effort changed and what would have sold anyway.

02 — The product

One short consultation. Three answers.

The shopper answers a few questions on a kiosk, a staff tablet or their own phone. FitFirst checks every piece on your floor against those answers, and returns the few that genuinely suit. The same result tells your salesperson what to bring and tells you what is working.

03 — What it does

Built around the moment a shopper says “show me something.”

For the shopper

Picks that explain themselves.

Each recommendation says why it suits: the colour against their colouring, the cut against the fit they asked for, the occasion, the budget. No scores, no percentages, no labels on anyone's body.

    04 — How it works

    From the stock room to the sale, in four steps.

    1. 1

      Catalogue the floor

      Photograph your pieces, starting with aged stock. AI drafts the tags, your team approves them, and each piece is live with its rack location.

    2. 2

      The shopper consults

      Section, what they are looking for, size, colouring and fit. A short guided flow on a kiosk, a staff tablet or their phone.

    3. 3

      Staff bring the picks

      The handoff code opens the session: what to bring, from where, and what to say. Outcomes are recorded as they happen.

    4. 4

      You see what moved

      Aged stock sold, compared with a control group that got no nudge, and a list of what shoppers wanted that you did not have.

    06 — What we hold to

    A recommendation is only worth something if it is honest.

    Shops live on returning customers. So the rules below are built into how FitFirst ranks and speaks. They are not settings that can be switched off.

    1. 01

      Never recommend what does not suit.

      Suitability is a gate, not a preference. Aged stock is favoured only among pieces that pass it.

    2. 02

      Rules you can read.

      Ranking is deterministic and explainable. An owner or a stylist can see why any piece was shown.

    3. 03

      Respectful language, always.

      Colouring is described in neutral terms. Shoppers choose fit goals and are never given a body label.

    4. 04

      Measured, not claimed.

      A control group and pass marks agreed in advance decide whether FitFirst earned its place in your shop.

    5. 05

      Fits how your shop already bills.

      Designed to sit alongside the billing software you use today, or to be the counter itself.
